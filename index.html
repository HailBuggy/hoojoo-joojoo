Unofficial Lexicon Entry

hoojoo joojoo

/ˈhuː.dʒuː ˈdʒuː.dʒuː/  •  “HOO-joo JOO-joo”
Coinage claimed by: Hailey Shaffer
First documented online: August 12, 2026
Prior use: Used in speech for several years before online documentation.

Definition

noun / adjective, slang Mystical, supernatural, spiritual, pseudoscientific, or otherwise woo-woo nonsense—especially an explanation that invokes magic, cosmic forces, energies, fate, destiny, or other unverifiable phenomena.

Usage

“Twin flames? Yeah, that’s some hoojoo joojoo shit.”

Origin

Hailey Shaffer reports using hoojoo joojoo conversationally for years before first documenting it online in 2026. She had not heard the phrase used by others before adopting it herself and believes she coined it independently.

This page documents that claim of independent coinage; it does not assert that no other person has ever independently formed or uttered the same phrase.

Semantic Range

Hoojoo joojoo is generally used with skeptical or humorous intent. It may describe elaborate spiritual explanations, cosmic relationship theories, dubious energetic claims, supernatural reasoning, or any belief system that feels suspiciously heavy on invisible forces and light on evidence.

Examples

“She said Mercury made him emotionally unavailable. That sounds like hoojoo joojoo to me.”
“I like crystals, but once we start saying they can fix my taxes, we’re entering hoojoo joojoo territory.”
This is an independent public-facing record of the term and its claimed origin.
